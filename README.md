# Dairy Business Performance Dashboard

## Overview

The Dairy Business Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze and monitor key business metrics in the dairy industry. It provides insights into revenue, sales performance, inventory status, customer locations, product demand, and brand performance through dynamic visualizations and KPI indicators.

## Features

* Revenue analysis and trend monitoring
* Units sold and average price tracking
* Product-wise revenue analysis
* Brand performance comparison
* Sales channel analysis (Retail, Wholesale, Online)
* Inventory monitoring through Products Needing Reorder
* State-wise revenue distribution
* Interactive slicers for Year, Product, Brand, Sales Channel, and Location
* KPI cards with key business metrics

## Dashboard KPIs

* Total Revenue
* Units Sold
* Average Price per Unit
* Total Products
* Total Brands

## Visualizations

* Revenue Trend by Month
* Revenue by Product
* Revenue by Brand
* Revenue by Sales Channel
* Products Needing Reorder
* Top States by Revenue

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI Desktop
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Power Query
* Microsoft Bing Maps (for geographic analysis)

## Dataset

The dashboard is built using the Dairy Goods Sales Dataset from Kaggle, containing sales, product, brand, inventory, and location-related information.

## Key Insights

* Identifies top-performing products and brands
* Tracks monthly revenue trends
* Highlights inventory items requiring replenishment
* Analyzes customer purchasing patterns across sales channels
* Provides geographic insights into revenue distribution
